# Industry Benchmarks Guide

## Overview
Benchmarks are industry standards and metrics from competitors and similar products. They help you understand where you stand and set realistic goals.

**Created by:** Ankit Saxena | **Purpose:** Educational Resource

---

## What Are Benchmarks?

- **Industry Standards**: Average metrics in your industry
- **Competitor Benchmarks**: Competitor's performance metrics
- **Historical Benchmarks**: Your own past performance
- **Best-in-Class**: Top performer metrics

---

## Key Benchmarking Metrics

### SaaS Benchmarks
- **CAC Payback**: 12-18 months
- **LTV:CAC Ratio**: 3:1 or higher
- **Churn Rate**: <5% monthly
- **NRR**: >110% (ideal)
- **Magic Number**: 0.75+ (growth)

### Mobile App Benchmarks
- **Day 1 Retention**: 25-35%
- **Day 30 Retention**: 5-10%
- **Uninstall Rate**: 20-25% per month
- **Session Length**: 2-5 minutes

---

## How to Benchmark

1. Identify key metrics
2. Research industry data
3. Analyze competitors
4. Set targets
5. Track progress
6. Adjust strategy
