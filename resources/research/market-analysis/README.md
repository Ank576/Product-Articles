# Market Analysis Guide

## Overview

Market Analysis is the process of gathering and analyzing data about your target market, industry trends, market size, growth potential, and competitive landscape. It helps product managers make informed decisions about market opportunities and product strategy.

**Created by:** Ankit Saxena  
**Purpose:** Educational Resource

---

## What is Market Analysis?

Market analysis involves understanding:

- **Market Size**: Total addressable market (TAM) and serviceable market
- **Market Growth**: Historical and projected growth rates
- **Market Trends**: Emerging patterns and shifts in the market
- **Customer Segments**: Different customer types and their needs
- **Market Dynamics**: Supply, demand, and pricing factors
- **Regulatory Environment**: Laws and regulations affecting the market

---

## How to Conduct Market Analysis

### Step 1: Define Your Market

- Identify the industry or product category
- Define geographic boundaries
- Determine customer segments
- Set analysis scope and timeframe

### Step 2: Gather Market Data

**Primary Research**
- Conduct surveys and interviews
- Attend industry conferences
- Perform customer focus groups
- Analyze customer feedback

**Secondary Research**
- Review industry reports
- Analyze government statistics
- Study academic research
- Review financial data

### Step 3: Analyze Market Size

**Total Addressable Market (TAM)**
- Total revenue opportunity in the market
- Calculate: Number of potential customers × Average transaction value

**Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM)**
- The portion of TAM you can realistically target
- Consider geographic, demographic, and product limitations

**Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)**
- Realistic market share you can capture in 3-5 years

### Step 4: Identify Market Trends

- Technology adoption patterns
- Consumer behavior shifts
- Regulatory changes
- Economic indicators
- Demographic trends

### Step 5: Segment the Market

**Geographic Segmentation**
- Analyze markets by location
- Consider regional differences

**Demographic Segmentation**
- Age, gender, income, education
- Family status, profession

**Psychographic Segmentation**
- Values, interests, lifestyle
- Purchasing behavior patterns

**Behavioral Segmentation**
- Usage rate, brand loyalty
- Benefit sought

### Step 6: Create Market Positioning

- Define your value proposition
- Identify target customer segments
- Determine pricing strategy
- Plan distribution channels
- Develop marketing approach

## Key Market Analysis Frameworks

### PESTLE Analysis

**Political**
- Government policies and regulations
- Trade agreements
- Political stability

**Economic**
- Growth rate, inflation, interest rates
- Employment levels
- Currency fluctuations

**Social**
- Cultural trends and values
- Demographics
- Consumer attitudes

**Technological**
- Technology adoption rates
- Innovation in the industry
- Automation potential

**Legal**
- Regulations and compliance
- Intellectual property laws
- Employment laws

**Environmental**
- Sustainability trends
- Climate impact
- Resource availability

### Porter's Five Forces

1. **Threat of New Entrants**
   - Barriers to entry
   - Capital requirements
   - Brand loyalty

2. **Bargaining Power of Suppliers**
   - Number of suppliers
   - Supplier switching costs
   - Supplier concentration

3. **Bargaining Power of Customers**
   - Number of customers
   - Customer switching costs
   - Availability of alternatives

4. **Threat of Substitute Products**
   - Alternative solutions
   - Price sensitivity
   - Product differentiation

5. **Competitive Rivalry**
   - Number of competitors
   - Industry growth rate
   - Product differentiation

### Market Sizing Methodologies

**Top-Down Approach**
- Start with total addressable market
- Estimate percentage you can capture
- Calculate revenue potential

**Bottom-Up Approach**
- Count individual customers/units
- Multiply by average revenue per user
- Sum to total market size

**Value Chain Approach**
- Analyze each step in the value chain
- Estimate market size for each step
- Consolidate for total market

## Market Analysis Metrics

- **Market Growth Rate**: Year-over-year percentage growth
- **Market Share**: Your product's percentage of total market
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**: Cost to acquire one customer
- **Lifetime Value (LTV)**: Total revenue from one customer
- **Market Penetration**: Current sales vs. market potential
- **Customer Churn Rate**: Percentage of customers lost

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Overestimating Market Size**: Be realistic about addressable market
2. **Ignoring Market Trends**: Stay updated on industry changes
3. **Poor Data Quality**: Use reliable sources
4. **Not Analyzing Competitors**: Understand competitive landscape
5. **Focusing Only on Current Market**: Look at future opportunities
6. **Inadequate Customer Research**: Talk to actual customers
7. **Ignoring Regulatory Changes**: Monitor legal environment
8. **Not Revisiting Analysis**: Update regularly as market changes
