# User Research Guide

## Overview

User Research is a critical component of product development that involves collecting qualitative and quantitative data directly from your target users. It helps you understand user behaviors, needs, pain points, and motivations to inform product decisions.

**Created by:** Ankit Saxena  
**Purpose:** Educational Resource

---

## What is User Research?

User research encompasses various methodologies to gather insights about your users:

- **Qualitative Research**: Understanding why users behave a certain way
- **Quantitative Research**: Measuring how many users exhibit certain behaviors
- **Behavioral Research**: Observing actual user actions
- **Attitudinal Research**: Understanding what users think and feel

---

## How to Conduct User Research

### 1. Define Your Research Goals
- Identify what questions you need answered
- Define your target audience
- Set success metrics

### 2. Choose Research Methods

#### Qualitative Methods
- **User Interviews**: One-on-one conversations with users
- **Focus Groups**: Group discussions with 6-10 users
- **Usability Testing**: Observing users interact with your product
- **Contextual Inquiry**: Observing users in their natural environment

#### Quantitative Methods
- **Surveys**: Collect data from large user groups
- **Analytics Review**: Analyze user behavior data
- **A/B Testing**: Compare two versions of your product

### 3. Recruit Participants
- Use screener surveys to identify suitable participants
- Aim for 5-8 participants for qualitative studies
- Ensure diversity in your sample

### 4. Conduct the Research
- Prepare clear research guides
- Ask open-ended questions
- Listen more than you talk
- Take detailed notes

### 5. Analyze Findings
- Identify themes and patterns
- Create user personas
- Document key insights
- Share findings with stakeholders

### 6. Take Action
- Translate insights into actionable recommendations
- Prioritize changes based on impact
- Implement changes
- Measure results

## Key Methodologies

### User Interviews
One-on-one discussions to understand user perspectives
- **Duration**: 30-60 minutes
- **Best for**: Understanding motivations and pain points
- **Sample size**: 5-8 participants

### Surveys
Quantitative method to gather data from many users
- **Distribution**: Online platforms, email, in-app
- **Best for**: Validating hypotheses at scale
- **Sample size**: 50-500+ respondents

### Usability Testing
Observing users interact with your product
- **Duration**: 30-45 minutes per session
- **Best for**: Identifying usability issues
- **Sample size**: 5-8 participants

### Focus Groups
Group discussions with 6-10 users
- **Duration**: 60-90 minutes
- **Best for**: Exploring ideas and getting group perspectives
- **Cost**: Moderate to High

---

## Best Practices

1. **Keep Questions Open-Ended**: Avoid yes/no questions
2. **Avoid Leading Questions**: Don't suggest answers
3. **Record Sessions**: Get permission and record for reference
4. **Be Neutral**: Don't show bias or defend your product
5. **Follow-up**: Ask "Why?" multiple times
6. **Diverse Participants**: Include users with different backgrounds
7. **Act on Findings**: Close the loop by implementing changes

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Confirmation Bias**: Don't only talk to users who agree with you
2. **Small Sample Size**: Ensure adequate sample for validity
3. **Biased Questions**: Use neutral, unbiased language
4. **Not Listening**: Focus on understanding, not pitching
5. **Not Recording Insights**: Document everything
6. **Ignoring Outliers**: Edge cases can reveal important patterns
7. **No Follow-up**: Always close the loop

## Questions to Ask Users

### Understanding Needs
- What challenges do you face when...?
- How do you currently solve this problem?
- What frustrates you most about...?
- What would make your life easier?

### Understanding Usage
- Walk me through how you would use this
- What did you expect to happen?
- Where did you look for...?
- Why did you click on...?

### Understanding Feelings
- How does this make you feel?
- What's your overall impression?
- Would you use this regularly? Why/why not?
- Who would benefit from this?
