# Product Management Tools

## Overview
Essential tools for product managers to design, analyze, and manage products efficiently.

**Created by:** Ankit Saxena | **Purpose:** Educational Resource

---

## Tool Categories

### Research & Analytics
- **Google Analytics**: User behavior tracking
- **Mixpanel**: Product analytics
- **Amplitude**: User engagement analytics
- **Hotjar**: Session recording & heatmaps

### Design & Prototyping
- **Figma**: Collaborative design tool
- **Adobe XD**: UI/UX design
- **Sketch**: macOS design tool
- **Miro**: Whiteboarding & ideation

### Roadmap & Planning
- **Jira**: Agile project management
- **Asana**: Task management
- **Monday.com**: Workflow management
- **ProductBoard**: Product roadmapping

### Documentation
- **Notion**: All-in-one workspace
- **Confluence**: Team documentation
- **Google Docs**: Collaborative documents

### Communication
- **Slack**: Team messaging
- **Loom**: Video recording
- **Miro**: Collaborative whiteboard
