# Product Management Templates

## Overview
Reusable templates for common product management documents and processes. Save time and ensure consistency across your organization.

**Created by:** Ankit Saxena | **Purpose:** Educational Resource

---

## Template Types

### Strategic Templates
- **PRD Template**: Product Requirements Document
- **Strategy Template**: Product strategy framework
- **OKR Template**: Objectives & Key Results
- **Roadmap Template**: Product roadmap

### Research Templates
- **User Interview Guide**: Interview structure
- **Survey Template**: Customer survey
- **User Persona Template**: Persona document
- **Competitive Analysis Template**: Competitor research

### Design Templates  
- **User Flow Template**: User journey maps
- **Wireframe Template**: Lo-fi mockups
- **Design Spec Template**: Design specifications
- **A/B Test Template**: Experiment design

### Metrics Templates
- **Dashboard Template**: Metrics dashboard
- **Report Template**: Monthly report
- **Retention Cohort**: Cohort analysis
- **Goal Setting Template**: Goal framework

---

## Using Templates

1. Download template
2. Customize for your product
3. Share with team
4. Iterate based on feedback
5. Version control
