## 11. Risks & Mitigation

### Product Risk
| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| High default rates | Strict underwriting, diversified portfolio |
| Low adoption | Strong UX, merchant partnerships, incentives |
| Competitive pressure | Unique features, customer loyalty, pricing |

### Regulatory Risk
| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| License denied | Engage regulators early, pre-compliance |
| Regulation changes | Legal monitoring, flexible architecture |
| Compliance violations | Strong internal controls, audits |

### Operational Risk
| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| System outages | Redundancy, monitoring, backup systems |
| Fraud/theft | Advanced detection, fraud team, monitoring |
| Data breaches | Encryption, security audits, insurance |
