## 📚 Writing Standards

Articles in this repository follow these guidelines:
- **Depth**: Minimum 1000-2000 words for comprehensive coverage
- **Examples**: Include real-world examples and case studies
- **Actionable**: End with take-away frameworks or checklists
- **Clarity**: Written for product managers of all experience levels
- **Data-driven**: Support claims with research and metrics

## 🤝 Contributing

## 📄 Article Template

Each article should include:
- **Title**: Clear, descriptive heading
- **Intro**: Hook and problem statement
- **Body**: Detailed analysis with examples
- **Framework**: Applicable principles or checklist
- **Conclusion**: Key takeaways
- **Resources**: Links and references
